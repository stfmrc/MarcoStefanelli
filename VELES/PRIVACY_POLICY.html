VELES — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Publisher / data controller: stfmrc

Privacy and support contact: stfmrc.dev@gmail.com

What the app stores

VELES stores the food records, calorie and macronutrient values, meal entries, portions, daily targets and body measurements that you choose to enter. This information is stored in a database on your device. The application does not require an account or transmit these records to a VELES-operated server in the supplied app version.

Why the data is used

Your information is used locally to display food lists, nutritional summaries, history and approximate food-related greenhouse-gas estimates. Emissions estimates depend on the dataset and may omit unmatched foods.

Third parties and optional tips

The supplied application does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Optional support tips are processed as in-app purchases by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and payment-related information under its privacy policies; VELES verifies transactions. No app features depend on purchasing a tip.

Files, export and device backups

CSV imports read files you explicitly choose. You can export and share CSV files containing food, meal and measurement data, or export the full dataset as a readable JSON file. You choose where to save or share these files. Recipients and cloud storage locations selected by you may retain exported data under their own terms. iOS may include local app data in device or iCloud backups under your system settings; these backups are controlled by Apple and your device preferences.

Retention, deletion and choices

Local data remains until you edit or delete it. Use More → Your data → Delete all local data to erase the locally stored records, or remove the app. Deleting app data does not remove copies you exported or copies retained in device backups. No HealthKit, contacts, or location access is requested by this version. For questions about data practices, use the public contact above.

Policy changes

This policy is specific to the released VELES version and should be updated before any material changes to data handling. Updates to the policy will be published at this URL.
